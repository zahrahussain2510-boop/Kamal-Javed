02 / A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Design instinct.
Developer mindset.

I’m Kamal Javed, a web designer and developer based in Pakistan. My work lives between a strong visual idea and the front end detail that makes it real.

VISUAL DIRECTION

I’m drawn to strong typography, confident contrast and layouts with a clear point of view. Every choice should make the experience more memorable and easier to follow.

FRONT END CRAFT

I build with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, giving each design responsive behavior, purposeful interaction and the care it needs across screen sizes.

MY APPROACH

Curious, hands on and detail focused. I like shaping the full experience, from the first visual direction to the final spacing, transitions and small-screen polish.
